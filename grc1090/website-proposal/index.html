Home

Welcome to Pearl & Bloom! We serve refreshing milk teas, fruit teas, matcha, and specialty drinks made just the way you like them. Whether you need a caffeine boost, a sweet treat, or a new go-to drink, there is always something waiting for you to sip and enjoy.

Menu

Find your new favorite sip! Choose from our selection of milk teas, fruit teas, matcha, and specialty drinks. Customize your drink with your choice of sweetness, ice level, and toppings.

Brown Sugar Milk Tea — Classic milk tea with brown sugar syrup and tapioca pearls.

Strawberry Matcha — Creamy matcha layered with sweet strawberry.

Classic Matcha — The drink you know and love. Creamy, rich, and full of the earthy but sweet matcha flavor you love.

Peach Jasmine Tea — Refreshing jasmine tea with sweet peach flavor

Mango Green Tea — A little tropical, a lotta juicy, sweet, tangy mango blended with a lovely green tea
